GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
to know.

dlsta helps you find a direct link to a public Instagram photo, Reel or video. The file comes from Instagram’s CDN—not through our server—and you don’t need to sign in to Instagram.

Only save content you have permission to use. dlsta is an independent project and isn’t affiliated with Instagram or Meta.

Can I save Instagram photos and Reels?

dlsta supports public photo posts, Reels and videos when Instagram makes the media available. For a carousel, select each available photo or video. Some items may be unavailable.

Why won’t a link work?

Private, deleted, restricted or temporarily blocked posts can’t be resolved. Instagram can also change how public media is served. Direct links can expire, so try finding the post again.

How do I save the media?

Open or copy the direct link. If your browser displays the photo or plays the video, use its save or download option. The file is served by Instagram’s CDN.
